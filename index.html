Frequently Asked Questions

Are the codes genuine?

Yes. Every code is 100% genuine and sourced only from authorized distributors and official brand partners — never from the grey market. If a code ever fails, we replace it or refund you in full.

How fast do I get my code?

Instantly. The moment your Mobile Money payment is confirmed, your code appears on the screen. We also send it to your email and WhatsApp so you never lose it.

Do I need a credit card or bank account?

No! All you need is a Mobile Money wallet — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha — with enough balance for your card.

Which brands can I buy?

Streaming & TV (Netflix, DSTV, GOtv, Showmax), music (Spotify, Apple Music), app stores (Google Play, Apple/iTunes), gaming credit (Free Fire, PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends, Roblox) and general gift cards. We add more brands as the pilot grows.

How do I redeem my code?

Every code comes with simple step-by-step redeem instructions for that exact brand — shown on screen and sent to your email and WhatsApp. It usually takes under a minute.

What if my code doesn't work?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number. If a genuine code fails to redeem, we replace it or refund you in full to your mobile money wallet.

Need help?

Orders, codes, redeeming or refunds — chat with our team on WhatsApp, 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00.
