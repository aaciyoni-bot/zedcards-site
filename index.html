Instantly. The moment your Mobile Money payment is confirmed, your code appears on the screen. We also send it to your email and WhatsApp so you never lose it.

No! All you need is a Mobile Money wallet — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha — with enough balance for your card.
