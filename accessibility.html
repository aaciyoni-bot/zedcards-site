Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 29 July 2026
ZedCards (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED) aims to be usable by as many people as possible and targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML, headings and labelled form fields.
  • Keyboard navigation with visible focus.
  • Readable colour contrast; meaning not by colour alone.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Responsive layout for phone, tablet and desktop.

Known limitations

Some third-party widgets (payment, fonts) may not fully meet our standards; we improve them over time.
